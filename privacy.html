Tally Butler Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

Tally Butler is a personal bookkeeping application that helps you record income, expenses, recurring transactions, reminders, currency conversions, and financial insights.

Information stored in the app

Tally Butler may store information that you enter into the app, including transaction amounts, dates, currencies, categories, sub-categories, profiles, notes, recurring transaction settings, and reminder settings.

This information is stored locally on your device unless you enable iCloud Sync.

iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud Sync, your bookkeeping records are stored in your private iCloud account using Apple CloudKit. Tally Butler does not operate its own financial database and does not have access to your private iCloud records.

iCloud data is handled according to Apple's privacy policies and your Apple Account settings.

Exchange rates

When automatic exchange-rate updates are enabled, Tally Butler sends the selected base currency to an exchange-rate service to retrieve current conversion rates. The app does not send your transaction history, account details, profile names, or personal notes to the exchange-rate service.

Exchange rates may be retrieved from third-party services, including ExchangeRate API and ExchangeRate.fun. Please review the applicable provider terms and privacy policies for more information.

Subscriptions and payments

Tally Butler Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Tally Butler does not receive or store your payment card details.

Subscription status is checked using Apple's StoreKit services so that the app can provide access to purchased features and restore purchases across your devices.

Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device. Reminder content is not sent to Tally Butler servers.

CSV import and export

CSV files are selected, created, and handled by you. Tally Butler does not upload your CSV files to a developer-operated server.

Analytics and advertising

Tally Butler does not display advertising and does not sell your personal or financial information.

Tally Butler does not use third-party advertising trackers or analytics services.

Data retention and deletion

You can delete transactions, profiles, categories, and other records from within the app. You can also remove locally stored app data by deleting the app from your device.

If iCloud Sync is enabled, iCloud records are managed through your Apple Account and iCloud settings.

Children's privacy

Tally Butler is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
