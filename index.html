Why Tally Butler

Many finance apps rely on bank connections, advertising, or complex budgeting systems. Tally Butler is designed for people who prefer a simpler manual approach and want control over what they record. It is a practical tool for personal bookkeeping without unnecessary complexity.

Common questions

  • How do I restore my subscription? Open Tally Butler, go to Settings, select Unlock Pro, and tap Restore Purchases.
  • How do I change my currency? Open Settings, select Main Currency, and choose your preferred currency.
  • How do I export my data? Open Settings and select Export Transactions as CSV.
  • How does iCloud sync work? When enabled, your records sync through your private iCloud database associated with your Apple Account.
