Common questions

How do I restore my subscription?

Open Tally Butler, go to Settings, select Unlock Pro, and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I change my currency?

Open Settings, select Main Currency, and choose your preferred currency.

How do I export my data?

Open Settings and select Export Transactions as CSV.

How does iCloud sync work?

When enabled, your records sync through your private iCloud database associated with your Apple Account.

Privacy

Tally Butler is designed to keep your bookkeeping data private. You can export your records whenever you need.
